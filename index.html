PDF Text Editor

Drop a PDF here

or click to choose a file. Everything stays in your browser.

  1. Click any text on the page to edit it.
  2. Change or delete words. Press Enter or click away when done.
  3. Use Remove to delete a whole line of text.
  4. Click Download PDF to save the edited file.

My documents

PDFs you open and every edit are saved automatically in this browser. Use Export backup now and then to keep a copy (for example in Google Drive) or to move your documents to another device.
